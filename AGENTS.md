### Nashik
- HAL Ozar builds the Su-30 MKI; Mahindra, Bosch, ABB, Schneider, CEAT and VIP have facilities in Nashik
- 2,500+ registered precision-engineering MSMEs
- ~90% of India's wine; more than half of India's wineries

### Benchmark: Prayagraj Maha Kumbh 2025 (UP)
- 45 days (13 Jan – 26 Feb 2025); 66+ crore pilgrim visits
- ₹3 lakh Cr+ economic activity (CAIT); ₹25,000 Cr+ state revenue
- ODOP pavilion: ~6,000 sqm, ~75 GI products, ~₹35 Cr sales (₹4.3 Cr in 2019)
- ₹7,500 Cr+ civic infrastructure spend plus ₹1,500 Cr on Mela arrangements; the temporary tent city left no industrial asset behind
- UPGIS 2023: ₹33.5 lakh Cr pledged → ~₹10 lakh Cr grounded, 14,000+ projects and 33.5 lakh jobs within ~12 months

### Maharashtra
- FY26 GSDP ₹51 lakh Cr (~$615 Bn), 14.3% of India's GDP, growing 7.9%
- Davos 2025: ₹15.70 lakh Cr, 54 MoUs, 75% grounded within a year
- Davos 2026: ₹30 lakh Cr in MoUs, ~40 lakh jobs, 18 countries
- FY25 FDI ₹1,64,875 Cr, 39% of India's total
- Vision: a $1T economy by 2030 and $5T by 2047

Sources cited in the deck: Maharashtra Economic Survey 2025-26; CMO statements on Davos 2025 & 2026; PRS Legislative Research; CAIT.
